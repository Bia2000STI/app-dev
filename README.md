
# My Favorite Cartoon: Tom and Jerry

## About the Movie
My favorite cartoon is **Tom and Jerry**. It is a funny animated series about a cat named Tom and a mouse named Jerry. They always chase each other and get into funny situations.

## Main Characters
- **Tom** - A cat who always tries to catch Jerry.
- **Jerry** - A clever mouse who often escapes from Tom.

## Why I Like It
I like *Tom and Jerry* because it is funny, entertaining, and enjoyable to watch. Their funny actions always make me laugh.

## My Favorite Quote
> "The fun never ends when Tom chases Jerry!"

## Favorite Things About the Cartoon
1. Funny moments
2. Creative tricks
3. Interesting characters
4. Classic animation

## Conclusion
**Tom and Jerry** is one of my favorite cartoons because it always makes me happy and laugh.
